Contact & Contribute

“Anyone can code, but not everyone will code.”

We welcome everyone — programmers and non-programmers alike — to share ideas, report bugs, or even submit your own modified version of A++.

How to contribute

  1. Download the latest version of A++ from our download section.
  2. Use it, experiment, and note any suggestions or changes you'd like to see.
  3. If you have modified the code, describe what you changed and why.
  4. Send an email to aplus@vishnutheertha.in (or your chosen email) with your feedback or attached code.

📧 Email us at: aplus@vishnutheertha.in

Even if you are not a programmer, your suggestions are valuable. We believe that good ideas come from anyone, anywhere.

All contributions will be reviewed by the maintainer. If your code or idea is used, you will be credited on the Development page.
