Contact & Contribute

“Anyone can code, but not everyone will code.”

We welcome everyone — programmers and non‑programmers alike — to share ideas, report bugs, or even submit your own modified version of A++. Your voice matters, and every suggestion helps make A++ better for everyone.

📬 How to contribute

  1. Download the latest version of A++ from our download section.
  2. Use it, experiment, and note any suggestions, changes, or new features you'd like to see.
  3. If you have modified the code yourself, describe what you changed and why.
  4. Use the form below or send an email directly to aplusplus.teamsupport@gmail.com.

✨ Even if you are not a programmer, your ideas are valuable. We believe that good ideas come from anyone, anywhere.

📢 What happens after you send a message?
All contributions will be reviewed by the maintainer (Vishnu Theertha). If your code or idea is used, you will be credited on the Development page. We aim to reply within 5–7 days.
🔧 Want to share your own modified A++ version?
Great! Please include a clear description of your changes, and if possible, attach your modified aplus.py or a patch. We’ll test it, and if it fits the project vision, we’ll add it with full credit to you.
